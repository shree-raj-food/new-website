Comprehensive Dental Services

The Smilist of Hoboken provides routine and specialized treatments to keep your smile healthy, bright, and strong.

General Dentistry

Routine exams, cleanings, and dental x-rays to maintain long-term oral health and prevent dental issues before they start.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Enhance your smile’s aesthetic with tailored cosmetic treatments designed to improve shape, alignment, and appearance.

Emergency Dentistry

Unexpected dental emergencies are handled promptly with priority scheduling to relieve pain and restore your comfort.

Teeth Whitening

Professional tooth whitening solutions to lift discoloration and brighten your smile quickly and safely.

Permanent, natural-looking tooth restoration for single or multiple missing teeth to revive confidence and bite strength.

Braces & Invisalign

Modern orthodontic solutions to straighten misaligned teeth discreetly and effectively for all age groups.
